2. Purpose, audience and value
Sakkiman Japan Map Drill helps elementary school students (grades 4-6) in Japan prepare for the social studies section of junior high school entrance exams. Everything to memorize - prefectures, prefectural capitals, mountains, rivers, plains, climate, agriculture and industry, historical sites and World Heritage sites - is learned together with its location on a map of Japan. The app is in Japanese only.

3. How to use the main features (no login or sample files needed)
- Title screen: tap "はじめる" (Start).
- Course list: tap a course, e.g. 1 "県名タップ" (Tap the prefecture).
- Choose a region (e.g. 関東) or "全国の挑戦" (all 47).
- A question appears at the bottom (e.g. "埼玉県はどこ？" = Where is Saitama?). Tap the prefecture on the map. Some questions are 4-choice instead.
- After answering, an explanation appears. Tap "つぎへ" (Next).
- Hold the eye button to show prefecture names temporarily. Pinch to zoom the map.
- Top buttons: light/dark theme, BGM and sound effects on/off.
- "リセット" (Reset) at the bottom of the course list: erase progress, or back up progress as a line of text (copy) and restore it.

4. External services
None. All screens, images, sounds and fonts are bundled in the app, and it works offline. There is no server communication, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no AI. Progress is stored only on the device.

5. Regional differences
The app works the same in all regions where it is offered. There is no region-specific content.

6. Regulated industries / third-party content
Not applicable. Map data is based on Natural Earth (public domain). Statistics are from Japanese government sources (Ministry of Agriculture, Forestry and Fisheries, etc.), cited in the app data. The character illustrations and sounds were made for this app. Fonts: BIZ UDPGothic and Dela Gothic One (SIL Open Font License 1.1).

## 画面収録で撮る流れ（約1〜2分）

1. コントロールセンターの「画面収録」で録画開始 → ホーム画面のサッ記マンを起動
2. 「はじめる」→ コース一覧
3. 「県名タップ」→「関東」→ 2〜3問答える（目のボタンを長押し、ピンチで拡大も見せる）
4. 「ホーム」→ ほかのコース（例：火山、歴史の場所）を1問
5. 右上でダーク／ライト切りかえ、BGM オン
6. いちばん下の「リセット」→「記録をバックアップ」→ コピー → とじる
7. 録画終了
